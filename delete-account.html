Data deletion

Delete Your WingRadar Account and Data

WingRadar users can request deletion from the app if available or by contacting support.

In-app option

If available, open WingRadar, go to Settings, tap Delete Account, and confirm. If you are leading an active ride, deletion may be blocked until you transfer leadership or end the ride.

Request by email

Email support with the subject: WingRadar Account Deletion Request.

Include the Google account email used for WingRadar, approximate date of last use, and confirmation that deletion is requested.

What data may be deleted

Depending on what information exists in WingRadar systems, a deletion request may include WingRadar user profile or account data where available, rider name, account identifiers, active ride membership records, ride data associated with the user where available, movement or location records associated with active or retained ride data where available, local session state only when deletion is performed in-app, and related account records where available.

Verification

WingRadar may need to verify that the requester is authorized to delete the data before processing the request.

What may be retained

Limited records may be retained temporarily for security, abuse prevention, diagnostics, reliability, legal compliance, dispute handling, backups, or service provider logs before removal through normal retention processes.

Processing

We will review and process deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the request. WingRadar does not promise instant deletion or automatic deletion from all systems unless that behavior is implemented and available.

Contact

For privacy questions or help with deletion requests, contact support.
