FAQ

Do I need mobile data to use WingRadar?

Yes. Active ride visibility works best with a stable mobile connection.

Can I use WingRadar for car convoys?

Yes. WingRadar supports motorcycle, car, and mixed convoy coordination.

How many riders can join a ride?

Ride size may vary as WingRadar evolves. Keep groups practical and easy to coordinate.

How is my location used?

Location tracking is only used for live ride coordination while the ride is active. After the ride ends, the app will not collect tracking info.

Can I use POI and SOS alerts during a ride?

Yes. POI alerts help riders find fuel stations, charging points, and restaurants, while SOS alerts support rider safety during the active ride.

Which platforms is WingRadar available on?

Android release access is coming soon. Availability may vary as the product develops.
