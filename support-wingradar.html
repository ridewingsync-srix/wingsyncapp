Help WingRadar Keep Moving

WingRadar was created to help riders and drivers stay connected, coordinated, and informed during group journeys.

This project has been designed and developed independently for the riding and driving community. I am also a salaried employee building WingRadar alongside my regular work, funding much of its development and infrastructure personally.

WingRadar is not asking for charity. It is inviting people who believe in the project to help sustain its growth.

Visit Help Center Return to WingRadar

Every contribution helps support:

  • Server and infrastructure costs
  • Mapping and routing services
  • Testing across real devices and road conditions
  • Security, backups, and monitoring
  • Continued development and maintenance
  • Keeping the app accessible to more riders and drivers

Many people who could benefit from WingRadar are riders, drivers, clubs, families, and travel groups managing everyday expenses carefully. The goal is to keep the product practical and accessible without placing unnecessary cost barriers on the community.

Support is completely optional. Using WingRadar should never depend on making a contribution.

“If WingRadar has helped your group stay together, reduced confusion on the road, or simply feels like a project worth supporting, your contribution can help keep it moving forward.”
